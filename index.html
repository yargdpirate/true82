TRUE 82 — an NBA draft game built for the 82–0 chase

TRUE 82 is a free browser game: draft five NBA players from random franchise-and-era tickets, and a BPM-based season engine projects whether your lineup could run the table. It rewards roster fit — shooting, usage, defense, positional balance — not just stacking box-score legends.

How to play

Each of five rounds deals a random NBA franchise and decade. Draft a player who suited up for that franchise in that era and fill a two-guard, two-forward, one-center lineup, using any season of each player's career. The engine grades your five on advanced impact (BPM) and converts net rating into a projected 82-game record; every draft screen carries a HOW TO PLAY button with the full rules. Going 82–0 is possible. It is not likely.
