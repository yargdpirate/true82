# TRUE 82 FAQ

Quick answers about the game, the modes, and the engine. For the deeper
mechanics, see https://true82.net/how-it-works/.

## What is TRUE 82?

TRUE 82 (https://true82.net/) is a free browser-based NBA draft game. Each
round deals a random franchise-and-era ticket; you draft five players (two
guards, two forwards, one center), and a BPM-based season engine projects your
lineup's 82-game record. The goal is a perfect 82-0 season.

## Is TRUE 82 free?

Yes. TRUE 82 is completely free to play in any modern browser, with no account,
no download, no ads, and no cookies. There's an optional donate button if you
enjoy it, and that's the extent of the commerce.

## How do you play TRUE 82?

Draft a five-man roster with two guards, two forwards, and a center. Each of
five rounds gives you a random NBA team from a random decade; pick a player who
played for that team in that era. When the lineup is full, the engine simulates
the season and hands you a projected record.

## What ways can you play TRUE 82?

The four solo modes are Classic, Pro, Presti Mode, and Kaman Mode. Classic
shows full stat lines and lets you choose a player season; Pro hides the stats
and locks each player to a mystery season; Presti gives you a $50 cap against
prices that lie; and Kaman Mode is five Chris Kamans. Beyond solo play, Today's
Board and the weekly challenge give everyone the same setup, Duel is a
correspondence head-to-head draft, League turns weekly challenges into a
season, and Arena collects profiles, leaderboards, and run history.

## What makes TRUE 82 different from other 82-0 games?

The engine. TRUE 82 doesn't just add up counting stats: player value is built
from Box Plus/Minus, and the model adjusts for roster fit, penalizing
overloaded usage and weak positional defense while rewarding shooting. A lineup
of five ball-dominant legends will underperform four stars plus the right role
player.

## Why does TRUE 82 use BPM?

Box Plus/Minus estimates a player's per-100-possession impact relative to
league average from the box score, which makes it comparable across eras and
available for the whole modern record. That lets the game price a 1970s big man
and a 2020s guard on the same scale. It's also why the player pool starts in
1974, when the underlying stats begin.

## Can you actually go 82-0 in TRUE 82?

Yes. A strong enough lineup projects to a perfect record, and the game's footer
counts how many drafts have finished 82-0. In Classic, good drafting gets you
there regularly; in Presti Mode the salary cap makes perfection rare by design;
in Kaman Mode it is guaranteed.

## Does TRUE 82 work on mobile?

Yes. TRUE 82 is designed mobile-first: the whole interface lives in a single
phone-width column, and drafting is built around taps rather than drags. It
plays the same in a desktop browser.

## Where does the player data come from?

Player and team data comes from Basketball Reference and Stathead, maintained
by Sports Reference LLC. TRUE 82 is an independent fan project with no
affiliation or endorsement.
