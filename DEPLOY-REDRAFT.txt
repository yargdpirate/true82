SMOKE TEST AFTER DEPLOY (5 minutes)
-----------------------------------
1.  Load /, footer ends in v49.
2.  Home: THE REDRAFT door sits under Dynasty.
3.  FIRST THING, once, with the console open: visit /?redraft=2016 then tap
    through all nine class chips and watch for "[redraft] ... names not in
    live data, dropped:" lines. Each is a spelling to fix in SD_CLASSES
    (one array per class; entries accept "Name|Alternate Spelling"). A
    class plays fine with drops as long as 15+ resolve AND it can field
    three legal teams; otherwise the gate refuses and says which position
    the data is short on. 2009 is the one to watch: it is center-starved by
    design and doubles as a test of your position labels.
4.  DRAFT THE CLASS. Confirm: order strip shows your seat, rival picks land
    as visible beats about a second apart, their players grey out with
    TAKEN tags, the year dropdown works on your turn, and slot buttons in
    the tray disable with a reason when a pick is illegal.
5.  Finish the draft: podium with three records, winner stamped, your row
    outlined. RUN IT BACK reshuffles seats and starts clean.
6.  Try to grab a player a rival just took: the card should shake with a
    deny, not select.
7.  v49.1: the podium shows a receipts line per team (shooters X of 3,
    spacing, defense, usage) and board cards carry the engine's 3PT/GRAVITY
    chips. If a shooting-poor team posts a huge number, the receipts say
    exactly what it paid for the privilege.

WHAT TO WATCH WHILE PLAYING
---------------------------
  - Do the rivals read as GMs? MERCER should feel like a talent absolutist,
    QUINCY should visibly reach for fit. The knobs are three numbers per GM
    in SD_GMS (needW, scW, jitter).
  - Does the steal moment land? /avocado: showdown_state action=steal.
  - Pool size feel: 20 names for 15 picks means five leftovers. If final
    picks feel too easy, trim toward 18; too punishing, grow toward 22.
    One array.
