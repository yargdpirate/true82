v49.11 AMENDMENT - the screen was being repainted, and it remembers now
======================================================================
THE BUG (why it looked wrong on your phone and right in my tests):
decorate3dButtons() adds .presti-spin to EVERY button that does not opt
out via BTN3D_EXCLUDE, which painted both difficulty cards with the gold
raised-button skin: PICKUP became a gold slab, PRO became amber text on
amber. My render harness did not run the decorator, so it could not see
it. Both cards now carry .tm-flat (the documented opt-out, same marker
the vote arms and the widget IDK button use), the harness now runs the
real decorator, and h7 asserts the marker so this cannot regress.
The screen also uses the gate's own back control now instead of a .btn.

THEMING PUSHED
The two cards had to read as two different places, so PICKUP is now
genuinely daylight: warm asphalt, a low sun burning in from the upper
left, a thick chalk arc catching the light, chain-link shadow fading
down the top edge, a baseline highlight along the bottom. PRO went the
other way: near black, a hard spotlight cone falling from above the
wordmark, scanlines, bulb glow. Names went to 33px, body copy to full
white, fine copy tinted per court (warm sand on PICKUP, cool grey on
PRO).

REMEMBER MY CHOICE
A checkbox under the cards writes the choice to localStorage
(t82_redraft_diff_v1). If set, opening The Redrafted skips the screen
and goes straight to the class gate; the PICKUP/PRO pill there still
reopens it. The box shows already checked when a choice is remembered,
and unchecking then picking clears the memory while still applying that
pick. Difficulty analytics carry value 1/0 for whether it was
remembered. NOTE: this is Redrafted only. Classic is untouched, per
your instruction to hold it for a later pass.
