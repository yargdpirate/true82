2. ANALYTICS, ONE EVENT, SPLIT BY MODE

value: 1|0 (remember box checked), source: stored|store_failed|session}.
The Redrafted's old emit moved to the same event, so /avocado reads one
name and splits by mode. "source: store_failed" means the player asked
to be remembered and NO storage medium accepted the write.

3. THE REMEMBER CHECKBOX, HARDENED
Two mediums, self-healing: every write goes to localStorage AND a
one-year SameSite=Lax cookie; every read tries localStorage, falls back
to the cookie, and REPAIRS whichever medium lost the value (Safari's
7-day localStorage eviction and corporate storage policies are the
usual killers; the cookie survives most of them, and vice versa).
Writes read back and report success. Only "pickup"/"pro" ever
round-trip; garbage reads as null. Keys are per mode
(t82_redraft_diff_v1, t82_classic_diff_v1) and never cross. When NO
medium works, the box renders dimmed, disabled, and says "Not available
in this browser" instead of pretending. Visually the row is now a
full-width bordered panel that lights amber when checked.
A remembered Classic player skips the screen; a "PICKUP/PRO - CHANGE"
pill under the Classic button reopens it.
