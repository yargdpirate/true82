   Tap an arm: it latches AND pops (spring scale), a colored ring bursts
   off it, a +1 (or -1) floats up and fades. The tally swap is HELD until

   Working as designed: op=featured serves exactly ONE question per day,
   picked deterministically from the day number SERVER-side, so incognito
   cannot change it. Fresh questions (under 5 votes) serve on even day
   numbers, one per day. Your 24 landed (their priorities put them inside
   the serving window) and will cycle through the featured slot starting
   the next even day. Real bug found while proving it: the pool query was
   LIMIT 80 and the pool is now 104, silently making the 24 lowest-priority
   OLD questions unreachable forever. Raised to 200. If one-fresh-every-
   other-day is too slow a drip for 21 new questions, the rotation cadence
   is one expression in op=featured; say the word.

   the board tag. This is also the app-side half of the 77-5 slot
