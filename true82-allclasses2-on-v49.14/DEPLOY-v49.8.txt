1. 3PT / GRAVITY NOW WORK EVERYWHERE
   Cause was not legacy properties. op=roster creates only TWO questions
   per drafted player, chosen by a hash of name+season out of twelve
   traits, so whether a shooter question existed was a lottery. That is
   why CP3 and Mullin had arrows and Baron Davis, Luka and McAdoo did
   not. New worker op=engq mints the shooter question on demand using
   the SAME id formula op=roster uses (INSERT OR IGNORE, restricted to
   three-point-shooter and super-three-point-shooter, max five players
   per call, one confirming read so a retired row is never offered).
   The client calls it only for cards whose engine chip did not resolve.
   Fails soft: no answer means the chip keeps plain expansion, and no
   vote is ever offered against a question that does not exist.
