# TRUE 82

TRUE 82 (https://true82.net/) is a free browser game: draft five NBA players
from random franchise-and-era tickets, and a BPM-based season engine projects
whether your lineup could run the table. It rewards roster fit — shooting,
usage, defense, positional balance — not just stacking box-score legends.

## How to play

Each of five rounds deals a random NBA franchise and decade. Draft a player who
suited up for that franchise in that era and fill a two-guard, two-forward,
one-center lineup. The engine then converts your roster into a projected
82-game record. Going 82-0 is possible. It is not likely.
