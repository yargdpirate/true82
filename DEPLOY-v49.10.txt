THE SCREEN
Opening The Redrafted now asks DIFFICULTY? with two full-bleed court
cards. PICKUP is the blacktop at golden hour: asphalt wash, sunset rim
light, a chalk free-throw arc clipped into the corner, a chain-link
lattice along the top edge, the wordmark hand-tilted like court paint.
PRO is the arena tunnel on draft night: scanlines, a spotlight cone
from above, the wordmark in scoreboard bulb glow. Cards stagger in;
reduced-motion stills them. The intro asks on EVERY entry; a PICKUP/
PRO pill on the class gate reopens it; RUN IT BACK keeps your choice.
A live draft snapshots its difficulty like it snapshots its class, so
switching mid-flight never mutates a running draft.

PICKUP (the short board)
Curated headliner lists exactly as before. Peak season is the DEFAULT
(the season dropdown still works and still overrides).

PRO (the whole class)
Each class gains a deep tier that joins the board: about 140 authored
names across the nine classes, every one with at least one plausibly
viable (785+ minute) season, real draft position wired into board
order, undrafteds sorting after the picks by peak minutes. Darko at 2.
Thabeet at 2. Jonny Flynn one pick before Curry. Duncan Robinson,
Alvarado, Moochie Norris, the Junkyard Dog. Seasons stay randomized.
Names the live data spells differently are aliased (Hernangomez,
Labissiere, Maledon, Teodosic, Darko, Pietrus, B.J./Byron Mullens,
Kira Lewis Jr., Xavier Tillman Sr., Harry Giles III, Kevin Knox II,
Lonnie Walker IV, Danuel House). A name that resolves to no viable
season silently never appears; a name the data cannot resolve at all
logs to console as before.
