Five concept plates for reprinting TRUE 82 as a risograph: fluorescent ink, halftone dots, cream paper, and the slightly-off registration of a real drum press. The app already reaches for print, with tickets on the draft and a newspaper for the results, so these take that instinct all the way. Everything below is drawn live in your browser, one halftone dot at a time. There isn't a single image file on this page.

Plate III · The season

The Reel, Reprinted

Replaces: THE SEASON · GAME BY GAME, the month-by-month reveal.

The beats and the words stay exactly as they are. These month lines are the app's own, from a real 78–4 run. What changes is the paper: every game gets stamped. Wins land as solid Sunflower coins, losses as hollow Scarlet rings, so the record still reads without color.

Plate IV · The 81st win

81–0, Down One

Replaces: the Hot Hand, Presti mode at exactly 81 wins.

Your own decision log names the beat: “81–0, down in the 4th.” So stage it. The ball hangs at the top of its arc and the arena holds its breath until you take the shot. The Heat Check tier you spin would set the odds; here it's seven in ten.

Plate V · Results

The Shape of a Season

Replaces: the results reveal, and the image people share.

Every season prints as its own landscape. The waterline is .500: each win lifts the ridge a step, each loss drops it one and leaves a pink bead on the skyline. The sun's height is the final win percentage, and the inks cool as the record falls, until a losing year sinks below the water under a moon.

Hover the print for any game.

Exact
The strip along the bottom is the game-by-game record. The landscape is its shape.
Seasons
The 78–4 run is real; the other four are samples.

How it prints. Each plate is drawn once per ink as a tone layer, screened into halftone dots at that ink's own angle, tinted, and multiplied onto baked paper, the same pipeline as a riso drum. The engine was written from scratch for this page, following the approach in sevenevesai/riso-windowseat (MIT).

Inks. Colors are Riso's published ink swatches. There is no black ink: every dark is two inks overprinting.

Names. Franchise names identify teams only. No team logos are reproduced; the ticket motifs are original.

Data. Plate III uses the results and month lines of a real 78–4 run on a local build; its game dates are illustrative. Plates IV and V use sample data except the 78–4 season.
