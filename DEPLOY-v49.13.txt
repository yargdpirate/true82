TRUE 82 v49.13 - CLASSIC PRO DEALS BLIND
========================================
Files: app.js, index.html. Key: app.js?v=20260808-blindpro-v49.
BUILD_V stays v49. No D1 paste. No worker change. sim-core untouched.

WHAT CHANGED
Classic's PRO difficulty now hides the numbers, matching what the
standalone Pro mode has always done. One predicate, boardBlind(), owns
the rule for both surfaces so they can never drift apart:
  boardBlind() = MODE is pro, OR (MODE is classic AND the run's
  difficulty snapshot is pro).

BLIND MEANS THE WHOLE INFORMATION SURFACE, not just the stat line.
Four things go, because hiding only the stat line would have left the
numbers readable three other ways:
  1. The stat line under each card.
  2. The community label chips (CLTCH, RIM-D, GRAVITY). They are
     stat-derived and they identify the season, so they ride with the
     stats. The pool label pass now exits early: no chips injected
     after render, and no label fetch at all on a blind board.
  3. The OBPM and DBPM sort chips. Sorting by a hidden number reveals
     it ordinally, which is why the standalone Pro mode never had them.
  4. The label legend (i) button, which explains chips that are gone.

BUG CAUGHT WHILE BUILDING (would have shipped otherwise)
Tapping ANY sort chip calls applyMetricYears(true), and its force path
deletes every undrafted player's year override to restore engine-value
defaults. On a blind board that would have wiped the randomized seasons
and let the lazy seeder re-roll fresh ones on the next render: an
unstable board, and a free reroll for anyone who tapped Min repeatedly.
applyMetricYears now returns early on a blind board. Tested both ways.

STILL VISIBLE, DELIBERATELY
The season menu, also blind (change years at your own risk). The search
box. The team and era skips. Names, positions, teams, years. And the
results screen reveals everything: the engine grades your five with the
real numbers at the end, which is the whole point of playing blind.

The draft plaque now reads CLASSIC . PRO / NO STATS . TAP MENU TO
CHANGE SEASON so the player can see which rules they are under. The
HOW TO PLAY brief was rewritten: bullet one splits by difficulty and
bullet three scopes the BPM sorts to Pickup.

FLAG FOR YOU (product, not a bug)
With this change, Classic + PRO difficulty is now functionally the same
game as the standalone Pro mode tile on the home screen: no stats, no
labels, randomized default seasons, season menu still live. The Pro
mode tile's own rules text already describes exactly this. Two options
whenever you want to decide, no rush:
  A. Retire the Pro tile and let Classic's difficulty own it. Frees a
     home-screen slot and removes the naming collision I flagged last
     pass (a MODE called Pro next to a DIFFICULTY called PRO).
  B. Keep both and differentiate them, e.g. Pro mode keeps a curated
     short board while Classic PRO keeps the full board.
I changed nothing about the Pro tile.

QA on deploy
1. Classic, pick PRO: cards show name, position, team, year, nothing
   else. No chips. Sort row shows only Min and A-Z. No (i) legend.
2. Tap Min, then A-Z, then Min again: the years on the board must NOT
   change. That is the bug above.
3. Finish the run: results show full stats and the label chips as
   always, including the vote strips.
4. Classic, pick PICKUP: everything back, OBPM/DBPM sorts present.
5. A daily and a duel: full stats, house rules, no difficulty screen.
