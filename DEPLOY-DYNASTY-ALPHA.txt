PLAY IT (5 minutes)
  - Home: a new door under Presti reads "Dynasty \u00B7 how long can you
    keep it alive?" It opens the gate; BEGIN THE DYNASTY drafts Season 1
    under Classic rules with the strip reading SEASON 1 \u00B7 TARGET 76.
  - Bank a season (76+ realized wins): the results page leads with
    SEASON 1 BANKED, the five names in the rafters, and DRAFT SEASON 2.
  - In Season 2's draft, any banked player-season shows RETIRED and
    disabled in its year dropdown; a player whose banked year was his
    default opens on his best legal year instead. Tapping a banked row
    any other way shakes with "SEASON 2 \u00B7 TARGET 76 says no."
  - Miss the target: THE DYNASTY FALLS, the obituary with every banked
    roster, all-time record, SHARE THE OBITUARY, START A NEW DYNASTY.
  - Leave mid-run: the home door reads "Season N waits" and the gate
    resumes. END THE DYNASTY needs two taps.

QA PARAMS (app-side only)
  ?dynasty=1        open the gate on load
  ?dynasty=reset    wipe the run, land on a fresh gate
  ?dythr=40         override every threshold (fast loop testing; try 40
                    to bank easily, 82 to see the fall)
